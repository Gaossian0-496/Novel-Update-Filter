Novel Updates Filter

Novel Updates Filter is installed

It sorts, filters and scores the novels on novelupdates.com as you browse. Pin its toolbar icon first so the popup is one click away.

Pin the extension to your toolbar

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Find Novel Updates Filter in the list.
  3. Click the pin icon next to it so the icon appears in your toolbar.
🧩
The puzzle-piece icon (top-right of Chrome) opens the extensions menu. Click the pin next to Novel Updates Filter to keep it visible.

What it does

BucketsMark any novel Read, Not Finished, Not Interested or To Read from its card. The first three hide it from listings.
FiltersHide whole languages or genres from the popup. Changes apply to open tabs straight away.
Recommendation scoringEvery card gets a 1–10 score from how closely it matches what you read and rated. Click the score to see why.
To Read sidebarA panel on the right of listing pages keeps your To Read novels, with covers and a Start reading button.
ManagerA full-tab page to search, move, remove, export and import every list.
Infinite scrollListings keep loading as you scroll, paced so Novel Updates does not block you. The Request queue page shows what is loading.
