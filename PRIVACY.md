# Novel Updates Filter: Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 8 November 2026_

This policy covers the Novel Updates Filter extension for Google Chrome. The extension is unofficial and not affiliated with Novel Updates.

## What data the extension handles

- **Your lists and notes:** the novels you mark Read, Not Finished, Not Interested or To Read, with any rating and comment you add.
- **Your settings:** language and genre filters, minimum rating, score threshold and display options.
- **Public novel information:** titles, tags, genres, descriptions, cover image links and first chapter links, read from novelupdates.com pages so the extension can filter and score listings.

The extension does not collect your name, email address, account details, browsing history outside novelupdates.com, location, or payment information.

## How it is used

Only to provide the extension's features: hiding and filtering novels, scoring listings against your reading history, and showing your To Read list.

## Where it is stored

All of it is stored in your browser using Chrome's extension storage, on your device. It is not synced to other devices and is never sent to the developer.

## Sharing

None. Your data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. The only network requests the extension makes go to novelupdates.com, from your own browser, the same as browsing the site yourself.

There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no remote code.

## Export and import

Exporting saves a file to your computer only when you click Export. Importing reads only the file you choose.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. You can remove any novel from your lists on the extension's manager page. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data.

## Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received by this extension complies with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.
